Nick Gonzales / Aurekai & Bonfÿre

I build Aurekai
and Bonfÿre.

I'm building an operating business that can support many kinds of work: an independent retailer, a creator with a publication, a service company, a community or an online marketplace. Aurekai is the business people see. Bonfÿre is the software I build and use underneath it.

My background is in software and integration work: existing websites and databases, business operations, low-level code and AI tools that can help develop whole applications. Aurekai lets those projects share software, knowledge and development work.

The work

From existing systems to new products.

A lot of software work starts with something that's already there: a website, an old database, a company with customers, an online publication, a supplier's product list. The interesting work is figuring out what those things could do together.

BUSINESS SOFTWARE

Websites and back offices

I've worked on PHP, WordPress, SQL and business integrations, including systems where a migration means preserving years of records, reports and customer workflows. That experience informs Aurekai's business and agency tools.

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

People, creators and shops

Aurekai brings profiles, publications, communities, catalogues, media workspaces and shopping experiences into connected products. A publisher's work or a retailer's catalogue can be useful in more places than its original website.

NATIVE SOFTWARE

Bonfÿre

How the business works

The next project can build on the last one.

AI makes software faster to create. It's still expensive to learn a business, connect its systems, support customers and keep an application running. Aurekai is my attempt to turn that ongoing work into something the whole company can reuse.

A catalogue built for a retailer can also be useful to procurement and 3D room planning. Source handling for one publication can support reading rooms, channels and creator profiles. A native integration developed for a customer can become part of the wider platform. Each project can begin with work we've already done, giving us more room to focus on its customers and what makes it different.
